### Advanced Hypothesis: Indo-European Root Constraints and Metrological Tokens
The lack of flexible word-formation (morphology) rules in Proto-Indo-European roots is a direct consequence of **T (Tools)** and **O (Occupation)** constraints in early metallurgy. In the Dead Sea and early metallurgical networks, trade was governed by the **physical weight and mass of metal tokens/ingots** rather than abstract counting. Because the socioeconomic valuation relied strictly on constant material weight (metrology), the linguistic cognitive framework adapted by maintaining rigid semantic roots combined with weight indicators, bypassing the evolutionary need for complex grammatical word-formation mechanisms.
